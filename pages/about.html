About

Have You Ever Wondered is a weekly halacha paper by Rabbi Chaim Heinemann of the Cincinnati Community Kollel.

The Author

Rabbi Chaim Heinemann

Rosh Kollel, Cincinnati Community Kollel

Rabbi Heinemann is the newest Rosh Kollel of the Cincinnati Community Kollel, where he has been since 2011. He looks for new ways to enhance the staff’s own learning and to create new opportunities for the community to benefit from the Kollel, and he is always ready to help those in need of halachic guidance.

He directs the Kollel’s Staff Scholars in their morning studies. The Sunday brunches he started regularly draw dozens of participants from all backgrounds for entertaining and informative Torah learning.

Rabbi Heinemann holds semicha from several of Yerushalayim’s leading rabbanim. He is Cincinnati’s certified mohel, a trained shochet, and the treasurer of the Cincinnati Eruv.

The Paper

Each week’s paper takes one question, the kind you may never have thought to ask, and works through the halacha on a single page, with its sources in the footnotes. The papers are collected here by parsha and by topic.
